About our school

Priyanka Public School

Priyanka Public School is located in Mankari, Bulandshahr, Uttar Pradesh. We are committed to providing quality education and developing confident, responsible, and compassionate citizens through a balanced blend of academics, sports, and co-curricular activities.

Our school is guided by experienced educators who focus on concept clarity, communication skills, and strong values rooted in Indian culture.
